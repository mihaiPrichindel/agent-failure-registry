# Failure registry: pathologies of working with AI agents

I run a small engineering system where most of the work is done by AI agents, on a math
education platform. This registry is the list of ways that work has failed on me.

Each entry is one named pattern. It carries the date it was first recorded, the incident
retold plainly, the guard that came out of it, and, with equal weight, what that guard does
not catch.

That last section is the point of the whole thing. A guard whose limits are not written down
is a guard that gets trusted past its range. These limits are not theoretical. Several of
these patterns survived the guard built against them, sometimes on the same day.

**Growth rule: one entry per incident, each with its declared limit.** Nothing here gets
marked resolved. Patterns are studied, not eradicated. Guards reduce exposure and shorten
detection. They do not cure.

## How this is written

The entries in this registry are drafted by AI agents working under mandate, inside the system
they describe. Research agents read the primary sources end to end. An adversarial verifier
tries to refuse every factual claim before publication, against files and line numbers. I read,
cut, and sign. Nothing is published without my explicit press.

The voice rule is strict. "I" refers only to acts I literally performed. Where an agent read,
ran, or measured something, the text says so.

This is not a disclosure appended for safety. It is the method this registry documents, applied
to itself.
