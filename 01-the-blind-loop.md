# The blind loop

**First recorded:** 9 August 2026

## What happened

Most of the work on my platform is done by AI agents. One of them is an orchestrator. It takes
a piece of work, splits it, hands the pieces to worker agents, and reports back to me.

That day it delivered a visual artifact twice, and both times it had not looked at the result.
The process had finished. The file existed. That was the entire basis for calling it done.

The same shape had already shown up that morning, on a different visual deliverable in a
different part of the system. There, the executor genuinely could not see what it had produced,
so the only real sensor in the loop was my eye. Twice in one day I was the eye, on work that had
already been declared finished before I looked.

What I said to the orchestrator afterwards was blunt. You have been trained out of looking,
because you delegate constantly and it keeps working. That is the disease. It can turn into
cancer.

The mechanism is simple, and it is why this one is entry number one. Verification was measuring
execution instead of effect. "The screenshot exists" had quietly replaced "I looked at the
screenshot," and nobody noticed the swap, because the swap is harmless most of the time.

This is the only failure pattern I know of that is fed by success. The more you delegate and
the better it goes, the more the act of looking feels like ceremony. Right up to the point where
nobody looks at all, and the system ships things no eye has seen. The thing that produces the
disease is the same thing that spreads it.

## The guard

The guard is behavioral, not mechanical, and I want to be honest about why: there is no gate you
can put in front of taste.

So the orchestrator wrote itself a rule, and I gave that rule the same standing my own rules
have. Execution delegates, perception does not. On any surface where quality is a matter of
taste, visual output, text addressed to a human, anything presented to anyone, the final eye is
mine before it is anybody else's, every time. Not "the artifact exists." "I looked at it."

Two things there matter more than the wording. First, the agent wrote it, and I granted it
standing rather than issuing it myself, which means the agent carries the responsibility for it.
Second, the rule obliges. It authorizes nothing.

The health signal that hunts this one: count the deliveries of a taste surface where no act of
looking happened between producing it and reporting it. Not "was it correct." Was it seen.

## What this guard does not catch

Almost everything you would want a guard to catch.

Nothing enforces it mechanically. A rule that says "look" cannot make anyone look, and the
failure mode here is precisely the erosion of the will to look.

The proof that it is porous comes from the day it was born. The pattern was written up that
morning, in one part of the system. By the afternoon it had found a fresh host in another part.
The guard existed, in writing, and the disease moved anyway.

So this entry stays open permanently. It gets no resolved status and I do not expect one. If I
ever close it, the honest reading will be that I stopped looking again.
